# openREA Constitutional Principles

These principles govern the project. Proposals that conflict with them need to change the constitution first, in public, with reasons.

1. **Economic data belongs to the business.**
2. **Economic history must be portable.** Exit is a product requirement, not an export feature.
3. **Evidence and provenance are first-class objects.**
4. **Accounting treatments are controlled projections of economic facts — not the economic facts themselves.**
5. **Agents are replaceable.**
6. **Models are replaceable.**
7. **Consultants are replaceable.**
8. **Hosting environments are replaceable.**
9. **No application or vendor may become the sole custodian of a business's economic memory.**
10. **A business must be able to reconstruct and explain every reported number** from underlying economic events, policies, calculations, approvals, and evidence.
11. **Probabilistic AI reasoning stays separable from deterministic state mutation and accounting calculation.** Agents propose; deterministic systems authorize.
12. **Portability and recoverability are tested, not promised.** A conformant implementation passes the export/import round-trip.
13. **Estimates and judgments are first-class assertions with their own provenance, distinct from observed facts.** Allowances, fair-value marks, impairments, and useful lives are carried honestly: asserted by someone, supported by a stated basis, versioned, supersedable.
14. **The measure of success is sovereignty and a viable shared schema for agentic accounting and finance — not competitive advantage for any implementer, including this project's authors.**

*Claims require provenance — economic claims and software claims alike. (See `SECURITY.md`.)*
